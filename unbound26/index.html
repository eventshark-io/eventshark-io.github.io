Survival notes6From people who have done the week before
  • Bring a water bottle. Fountains are scarce inside MCEC and bottles run six dollars.
  • Pack a sweater. The convention center runs cold all day.
  • Ugly comfortable shoes. You walk a half marathon a day between the hall and the Seaport.
  • Drink like you have a morning session. Two and a water. The after party is the trap, not the hotel bar.
  • One goal per day. One person, one session, one thing to learn. Save the rest for the hallway.
  • Save gas for Friday. The closing block is the least crowded and often the best.

Condensed from an r/hubspot thread. Contrast's yoga and run club are the light-exercise answer.
