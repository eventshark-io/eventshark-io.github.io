Field guide 03 From event goal to content library.

Six chapters. Ten working templates. A web edition plus the portable PDF.

01

Choose the event job

Owned event, sponsor booth, side event, or attendee capture. Start with what the content must do.

02

Design the content system

Connect recaps, testimonials, sessions, clips, photography, and sales assets to distribution.

03

Build the production brief

Lock the hook, audience, priority people, formats, guardrails, schedule, and backup windows.

04

Capture for the edit

Protect audio, multiple angles, crop room, standalone answers, branded environments, and b-roll.

05

Multiply in post

Organize by moment, lead with hooks, caption carefully, and release assets while the event is still relevant.

06

Distribute and reuse

Sequence hero edits, clips, photos, sales follow-up, nurture, ads, and quarterly recuts.
