Before, during, and after the event +
  1. 01

    Choose the event job

    Set the business goal before the shot list. Owned event, sponsor booth, side event, or attendee capture.

    Plan the content

    Connect recaps, testimonials, sessions, clips, and photography to where they will actually be used.

    Brief the crew

    Agree on the audience, priority people, formats, schedule, and backup windows.

    Capture for the edit

    Organize by moment, lead with a hook, and prioritize the first round of edits.
