About this conversation

Welcome to the EventShark Podcast, where we break down what's working in B2B content, LinkedIn strategy, and building a brand with personality .

In this episode, we sit down with Amanda Nielsen, B2B comedian behind Thot Leader Labs, a hilarious ecommerce merch brand built on corporate satire.

We talk about how she’s grown her personal brand to 17k followers on LinkedIn, monetized her sense of humor, and stayed employed the whole time... 😅
