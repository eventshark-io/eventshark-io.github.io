Winning Outbound in 2026, the Phone Comeback, Going All-In on Claude, “AI Slop” Problem, & Event Strategy (ft. John Eitel)

Jun 25, 2026 · 44 min · with John Eitel, CGO, Orum

OutboundSalesAI

About this conversation

If everyone has access to the same AI tools and can scale outreach faster than ever, how do you stand out from all the noise?

We sat down with John Eitel, Chief Growth Officer of Orum and a 7x sales leader, to talk B2B sales, AI adoption, and why human connection is becoming the real competitive advantage.

In this episode they get into:
- Cold calling making a comeback
- Running events that are prioritizing connection
- The cost of handing your whole sales team access to AI & more

AI makes good sellers better, but it doesn't create them. If you want to know how to use AI to sell smarter without losing the human side of selling, give this episode a listen!
