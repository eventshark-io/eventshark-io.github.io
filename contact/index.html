FAQs

What markets do you service?

Full-time teams in Austin, New York, and San Francisco - and shoots across the country. If your event is somewhere else, ask.

How fast do you turn around footage?

On a schedule we agree up front, typically starting the same week. You always know what’s coming and when.

Do you do single projects or retainers?

Both. Most teams start with one project and move to retainer once it proves out.

What industries do you focus on?

B2B companies in AI, SaaS, and logistics. No individual creators unless it’s for a B2B product.

Do you do photography too?

Yes - photography and headshots from the same crew, same day. Stills and video, one booking.

Can you turn around clips during the event?

Yes - our most-booked add-on. Keynote highlights can be live before the event ends.

Who owns the footage?

You do. Every deliverable is yours, organized in a library your team keeps pulling from.

We already have footage. Can you just edit?

Yes - webinars, podcasts, clip packages, and repurposing the library you already have.

How does pricing work?

Scoped to your goals. Event coverage usually starts as a one-day flat rate, deliverables defined up front, quote within days of the first call.
